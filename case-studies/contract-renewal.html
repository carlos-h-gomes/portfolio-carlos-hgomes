Case study 03 · Enterprise automation

Automating high-volume contract renewals

I designed an API-driven workflow that moved a repetitive, week-long contract process into a structured automation handling more than 100 renewals per month.

The challenge

A critical recurring process depended on manual batch work.

More than 100 contracts moved through renewal each month. Two people spent approximately one week per cycle coordinating expiration dates, templates, approvals, updates, and administrative follow-up. The process carried avoidable delay, rework, and human-error risk.

What I owned

The workflow from renewal trigger to recorded result.

  • Mapped the renewal states, dependencies, administrative variations, and approval steps.
  • Designed the integration boundary around Salesforce APIs and workflow orchestration.
  • Automated expiration triggers, template selection, approvals, and system updates.
  • Extended the pattern to related scope-change and administrative processes.
  • Kept the resulting records inside the system used by the operating team.

Architecture

A state-driven workflow around the system of record.

Renewal triggerExpiration and contract context read from Salesforce
Workflow rulesn8n evaluates path, required data, and template
Approval actionsCommercial and administrative steps follow the defined route
System updateOutcome and new contract state are recorded in Salesforce

Key decisions

Automate the coordination while preserving the business record.

  • Salesforce remains authoritativeThe workflow reads and writes the contract state where the operating team already works.
  • Rules are explicitRenewal, scope-change, and administrative paths are separated instead of hidden in manual judgment.
  • Templates follow contextThe workflow chooses the required action from contract information rather than relying on copy-and-paste.
  • Orchestration stays visiblen8n gives the team a workflow they can inspect and evolve as the process changes.

Outcome

A week-long monthly cycle became a short operational run.

The automated workflow reduced a full week of batch processing to approximately 30 minutes while handling more than 100 contracts per month. It returned capacity to two team members and reduced the operational exposure created by manual coordination.

Confidentiality: Contract content, internal rules, company identifiers, and production records are omitted. The public case focuses on the system boundary and conservative operational outcome.
